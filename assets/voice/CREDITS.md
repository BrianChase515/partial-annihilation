# Voice credits

War is Hell's battlefield cries (`cry_death_*.ogg`, `cry_hurt_*.ogg`) are recorded voices from
OpenGameArt. They were trimmed, filtered and level-matched for the game.

- `cry_death_1` to `cry_death_25`: "Death sounds (male) audio pack" by **VoiceBosch** (SoundBiter SFX library),
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  https://opengameart.org/content/death-sounds-male-audio-pack
- `cry_death_26` to `cry_death_33`: "Aargh male screams", collected by **congusbongus**,
  https://opengameart.org/content/aargh-male-screams
  - "Helmut Scream" by **creativeheroes**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), https://freesound.org/people/creativeheroes/sounds/84353/
  - "Male_Thijs_loud_scream" by **thanvannispen**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), https://freesound.org/people/thanvannispen/sounds/9432/
  - "human male scream multi" by **JohnsonBrandEditing**, [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), https://freesound.org/people/JohnsonBrandEditing/sounds/173944/
- `cry_hurt_1` to `cry_hurt_22`: "Damage sounds (male) audio pack" by **VoiceBosch** (SoundBiter SFX library),
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  https://opengameart.org/content/damage-sounds-male-audio-pack

The edited versions of the CC BY-SA sounds are shared under the same license.
